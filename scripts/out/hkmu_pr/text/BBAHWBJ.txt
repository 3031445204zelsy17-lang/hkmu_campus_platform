Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours in World Business
1. Period of Study
1.1. The programme Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours in World Business is
a four-year full-time programme which provides options for students to study at an
overseas partner institution in full-time mode. Students may also apply for an additional
year of overseas study which is optional and does not count towards the programme’s
requirements. Students who undertake this option will normally complete the
programme in five years.
2. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
2.1. To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Business Administration with
Honours in World Business through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1. obtain 120 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall
be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1. 60 credit-units of core courses as listed in Table 1;
2.1.1.2. 3 credit-units of core strategy course from Table 2;
2.1.1.3. 12 credit-units of concentration core courses from Table 3;
2.1.1.4. 30 credit-units of specialization courses from Table 4;
2.1.1.5. 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 5;
2.1.1.6. 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see
www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE for the updated list of GE courses);
2.1.1.7. All four Global Immersion Programme courses in Table 6;
and
2.1.2. must attain a cumulative GPA (CGPA) of 3.0 or above in the academic year
preceding their overseas study in order to be eligible to study at partner institutions.
Eligibility to apply is subject to specified requirements, such as achieving a
prescribed CGPA and meeting any other criteria set by the University. Students
who do not meet this requirement will be required to transfer to another
programme within the suite of BBA (Hons) programmes.
and
2.1.3. complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English
version NURS 1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version
NURS 1050NCF (精神健康急救課程) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students
admitted in and after 2025/26 cohort);
and
2.1.4. attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
