Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Education with Honours (Putonghua and Chinese Language Education) and
Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours (Chinese Linguistic Studies)
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to the following student
cohorts:
Year of Entry Admission cohort
Year 1 2024/25 and thereafter
Year 2 2025/26 and thereafter
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Education with Honours (Putonghua and
Chinese Language Education) and Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours (Chinese Linguistic
Studies) through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 150 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 36 credit-units shall be
at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall
be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 114 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry from Table 1;
1.1.1.2 6 credit-units of elective courses from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 3;
1.1.1.4 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 4;
1.1.1.5 3 credit-units of a periphery course from Table 5;
1.1.1.6 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 6;
1.1.1.7 6 credit-units of University English courses from Table 7; and
1.1.1.8 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF (精
神健康急救課程)) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after 2025/26
cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Education with Honours (Putonghua and
Chinese Language Education) and Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours (Chinese Linguistic
Studies) through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 123 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 96 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry from Table 1;
2.1.1.2 6 credit-units of elective courses from Table 2;
2.1.1.3 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 3;
2.1.1.4 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 4;
2.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 6; and
2.1.1.6 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses)
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
