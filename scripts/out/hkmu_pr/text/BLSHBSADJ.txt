Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours in Bilingual Studies with Applied Drama
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to the following student
cohorts:
Year of Entry Admission cohort
Year 1 2025/26 and thereafter
Year 2 2026/27 and thereafter
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours in
Bilingual Studies with Applied Drama through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 126 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall
be at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units
shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 102 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 Entry as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 3;
1.1.1.4 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 4;
1.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 5; and
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF
(精神健康急救課程)) (applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after 2025/26
cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours in
Bilingual Studies with Applied Drama through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 96# credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 78# credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 Entry as listed in Table 1
(except the courses marked with @);
2.1.1.2 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 2;
2.1.1.3 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 3;
2.1.1.4 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 4; and
2.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 5;
#Note: A student admitted through Year 2 entry may be required to take additional courses
marked with @ in Table 1 as determined by the School. Thus a student may need to
complete higher number of credit-units to fulfil programme requirements.
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
