Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Arts with Honours in English Language and Culture
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to the
following student cohorts:
Year of Entry Admission cohort
Year 1 2023/24 and thereafter
Year 2 2024/25 and thereafter
Year 3 2025/26 and thereafter
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Arts with Honours in English Language and Culture
through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 120 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall be
at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall
be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 69 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 12 credit-units of 2000-level major elective courses from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 12 credit-units of 3000-level major elective courses from Table 2;
1.1.1.4 6 credit-units of elective courses from Table 3;
1.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 4;
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of University English courses from Table 5;
1.1.1.7 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF (精
神健康急救課程)) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after 2025/26
cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Arts with Honours in English Language and Culture
through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 96 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-
level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 6 credit-units of from ENGL 1111AEF and ENGL 1121AEF;
2.1.1.2 51 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in Table 1;
2.1.1.3 12 credit-units of 2000-level major elective courses from Table 2;
2.1.1.4 18 credit-units of 3000-level major elective courses from Table 2;
2.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 4;
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Arts with Honours in English Language and Culture
through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 66 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-
level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 39 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in Table 1;
3.1.1.2 18 credit-units of 3000-level major elective courses from Table 2;
3.1.1.3 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 4;
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
