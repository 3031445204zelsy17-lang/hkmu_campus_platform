Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Education with Honours (Chinese Language Teaching) and Bachelor of
Language Studies with Honours (Applied Chinese Language Studies)
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document (from pages 1 to 4) is applicable to the
following student cohorts:
Year of Entry Admission cohort
Year 1 2023/24
Year 2 2024/25
For students admitted via Year 1 entry in or after 2024/25 cohort and via Year 2 entry in or after 2025/26
cohort, please refer to pages 5 to 8 for the programme requirements in this document.
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Education with Honours (Chinese
Language Teaching) and Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours (Applied Chinese Language
Studies) through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 153 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 36 credit-units shall be
at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall
be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 114 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry from Table 1;
1.1.1.2 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 3;
1.1.1.4 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 4;
1.1.1.5 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 5;
1.1.1.6 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 6;
1.1.1.7 3 credit-units of a periphery course from Table 7;
1.1.1.8 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 8;
1.1.1.9 6 credit-units of University English courses from Table 9; and
1.1.1.10 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses)
and
1.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.

1.1.1.2 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses)
and
1.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
