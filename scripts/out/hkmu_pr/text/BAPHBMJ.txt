Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Applied Psychology with Honours,
Bachelor of Business Management with Honours
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to students admitted in
2023 Autumn term and thereafter.
1. Programme Requirement – BAPHBMJ1
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Applied Psychology with Honours,
Bachelor of Business Management with Honours through Year 1 Entry (BAPHBMJ1), a student
shall:
1.1.1 obtain 150 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 36 credit-units shall
be at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units
shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 48 credit-units of business core courses applicable to BAPHBMJ1 in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 42 credit-units of psychology core courses from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 9 credit-units of business elective courses from Table 3;
1.1.1.4 12 credit-units of psychology elective courses from Table 4;
1.1.1.5 12 credit-units of synergy courses from Table 5;
1.1.1.6 12 credit-units of synergy courses from Table 6;
1.1.1.7 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 7;
1.1.1.8 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF
(精神健康急救課程)) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after
2025/26 cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
