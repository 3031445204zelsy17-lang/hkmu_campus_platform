Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours in
Finance and Financial Technology
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours
in Finance and Financial Technology through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 120 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall
be at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units
shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 60 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 3 credit-units of core strategy course from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 36 credit-units of concentration courses from Table 3;
1.1.1.4 6 credit-units of business elective courses from Table 4;
1.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 5;
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
1.1.1.7 All four Global Immersion Programme courses in Table 6;
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF
(精神健康急救課程)) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after
2025/26 cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Business Administration with Honours in Finance
and Financial Technology through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 90 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 36 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in Table 1;
2.1.1.2 3 credit-units of core strategy course from Table 2
2.1.1.3 36 credit-units of concentration courses from Table 3;
2.1.1.4 6 credit-units of business elective courses from Table 4;

3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Business Administration with Honours in Finance
and Financial Technology through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 63 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 12 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in Table 1;
3.1.1.2 3 credit-units of core strategy course from Table 2;
3.1.1.3 36 credit-units of concentration courses from Table 3;
3.1.1.4 3 credit-units of business elective courses from Table 4;
3.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 5;
3.1.1.6 GIP 300BEF and GIP 400BEF of the Global Immersion Programme courses in
Table 6;
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
