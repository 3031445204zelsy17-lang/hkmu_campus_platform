Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Science with Honours in Physiotherapy
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to students admitted in
2024 Autumn Term and thereafter.
1. Programme Requirement
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Science with Honours in Physiotherapy,
a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 159 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall be
at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall
be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 27 credit-units of Core Science courses in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 75 credit-units of Specialized Professional courses in Table 2
1.1.1.3 36 credit-units of Clinical Practicum in Table 3;
1.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 4;
1.1.1.5 6 credit-units of University English courses in Table 5
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 attain a grade “C” or above, or a “Pass” grade in all courses of the programme (otherwise
students must retake the respective course(s)) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students
admitted in and after 2025/26);
and
1.1.3 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF (精
神健康急救課程)) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after 2025/26
cohort);
and
1.1.4 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
