Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied Economics
Students admitted to Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours programme are required to declare the
stream they wish to pursue at the time prescribed by the University. The available streams are:
i. Ageing Society and Services Studies
ii. Applied Economics
iii. Applied Social Studies
iv. Global and China Studies
v. Politics and Public Administration
These programme requirements are applicable for students pursuing the “Applied Economics” stream for
the award of Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied Economics.
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied Economics
through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 120 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall
be at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units
shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 75 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 6 credit-units of 2000-level major elective courses from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 3 credit-units* of major elective courses from Table 3;
1.1.1.4 15 credit-units* of elective courses in specific area from Table 4;
*Among the 18 credit-units obtained from 1.1.1.3 and 1.1.1.4, students must
complete at least 6 credit-units at 4000-level.
1.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 5;
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of University English courses in Table 6;
1.1.1.7 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied Economics
through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 90 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 63 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in Table 1;
2.1.1.2 3 credit-units* of major elective courses from Table 3;
2.1.1.3 15 credit-units* of elective courses in specific area from Table 4;
*Among the 18 credit-units obtained from 2.1.1.2 and 2.1.1.3, students must
complete at least 6 credit-units at 4000-level.
2.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 5;
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours inApplied Economics
through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 63 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 42 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in Table 1;
3.1.1.2 3 credit-units* of major elective courses from Table 3;
3.1.1.3 9 credit-units* of elective courses in specific area from Table 4;
*Among the 12 credit-units obtained from 3.1.1.2 and 3.1.1.3, students must
complete at least 6 credit-units at 4000-level.
3.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 5;
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
