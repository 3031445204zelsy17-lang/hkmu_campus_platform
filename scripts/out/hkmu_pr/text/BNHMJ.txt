Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Nursing with Honours in Mental Health Care
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to students admitted in
2025 Autumn Term and thereafter.
1. Programme Requirement
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Nursing with Honours in Mental Health
Care, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 154 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall be
at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall
be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 102 credit-units of Theoretical courses (core) in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 2 credit-units of Theoretical courses (elective) from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 29 credit-units of Clinical Practicum courses in Table 3;
1.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 4;
1.1.1.5 6 credit-units of University English courses in Table 5; and
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see
www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE for the updated list of GE courses) at 2000-level;
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF (精
神健康急救課程)) (applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after 2025/26
cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain grade “C” or above or a “Pass” grade of the programme (otherwise students must
retake the respective course(s));
and
1.1.4 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of sub-
degrees.
Note: The minimum attendance requirement for theoretical courses (Table 1 and 2) is 90%. In addition,
School of Nursing and Health Sciences (N&HS) sets out specific attendance requirement for theoretical
courses having laboratory sessions.
