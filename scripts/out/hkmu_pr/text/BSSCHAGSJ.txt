Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Ageing Society and Services Studies
Students admitted to the Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours programme are required to declare
the stream they wish to pursue at the time prescribed by the University. The available streams are:
i. Ageing Society and Services Studies
ii. Applied Economics
iii. Applied Social Studies
iv. Global and China Studies
v. Politics and Public Administration
These programme requirements are applicable for students pursuing the “Ageing Society and Service
Studies” stream for the award of Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Ageing Society and Services
Studies.
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Ageing Society and
Services Studies through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 120 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall
be at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units
shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 72 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 9 credit-units of major elective courses from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 15 credit-units of elective courses in specific area from Table 3, of which at least
6 credit-units must be at 4000-level.
1.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 6;
1.1.1.5 6 credit-units of University English courses in Table 7;
1.1.1.6 9 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Ageing Society and
Services Studies through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 93 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 57 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in Table 1;
2.1.1.2 9 credit-units of major elective courses from Table 2;
2.1.1.3 15 credit-units of elective courses in specific area from Table 3, of which at least
6 credit-units must be at 4000-level;
2.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 6;
2.1.1.5 3 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Ageing Society and
Services Studies through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 66 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 42 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in Table 1;
3.1.1.2 9 credit-units* of major elective courses from Table 4;
3.1.1.3 6 credit-units* of elective courses in specific area from Table 5;
*Among the 15 credit-units obtained from 3.1.1.2 and 3.1.1.3, students must
complete at least 6 credit-units at 4000-level.
3.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 6;
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
