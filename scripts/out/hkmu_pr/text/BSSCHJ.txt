Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied Social Studies
(with no specialisation)
Students admitted to the Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours programme are required to declare
the stream they wish to pursue at the time prescribed by the University. The available streams are:
i. Ageing Society and Services Studies
ii. Applied Economics
iii. Applied Social Studies
iv. Global and China Studies
v. Politics and Public Administration
Students of the Applied Social Studies stream are further required to choose a specialisation at the time
prescribed by the Department of Social Sciences. The available specialisations are:
a. Applied Social Studies (with no specialisation)
b. Applied Social Studies (with specialisation in Criminology)
c. Applied Social Studies (with specialisation in Cultural and Heritage Studies)
d. Applied Social Studies (with specialisation in Social Policy and Youth Studies)
These programme requirements are applicable for students pursuing the specialisation of “Applied Social
Studies (with no specialisation)” for the award of Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied
Social Studies.
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied Social
Studies through Year 1 Entry with no specialisation, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 120 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall be
at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall
be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 69 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 12 credit-units* of major elective courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in
Table 2;
1.1.1.3 15 credit-units* of elective courses in specific area from Table 3;
*Among the 27 credit-units obtained from 1.1.1.2 and 1.1.1.3, students must
complete at least 9 credit-units at 4000-level.
1.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 6;
1.1.1.5 6 credit-units of University English courses in Table 7;
1.1.1.6 9 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied Social Studies
through Year 2 Entry with no specialisation, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 93 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-
level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 57 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in Table 1;
2.1.1.2 9 credit-units* of major elective courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in
Table 2;
2.1.1.3 15 credit-units* of elective courses in specific area from Table 4;
*Among the 24 credit-units obtained from 2.1.1.2 and 2.1.1.3, students must
complete at least 9 credit-units at 4000-level.
2.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 6;
2.1.1.5 3 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the Bachelor of Social Sciences with Honours in Applied Social
Studies through Year 3 Entry with no specialisation, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 66 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-
level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 42 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in Table 1;
3.1.1.2 9 credit-units* of major elective courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in
Table 2;
3.1.1.3 6 credit-units* of elective courses in specific area from Table 5;
*Among the 15 credit-units obtained from 3.1.1.2 and 3.1.1.3, students must
complete at least 9 credit-units from courses at 4000-level.
3.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 6;
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
