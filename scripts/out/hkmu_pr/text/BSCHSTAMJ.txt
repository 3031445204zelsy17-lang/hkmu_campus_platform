Year of Entry Admission cohort

For students admitted via Year 1 entry from 2024/25 to 2025/26, via Year 2 entry from 2025/26 to
2026/27 and via Year 3 entry from 2026/27 to 2027/28, please refer to Page 9 to 16 for programme
requirements.
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Science with Honours in Science (STEAM),
a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 120 credit-units* as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall
be at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units
shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 24 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry from Table 1;
1.1.1.2 24 credit-units of Integrated STEAM training in Table 2;
1.1.1.3 51 credit-units of elective courses from Table 3, of which at least 12 credit-units
shall be at 3000-level and at least 15 credit-units shall be at 4000-level;
1.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 4;
1.1.1.5 6 credit-units of University English courses in Table 5;
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
*Of the 120 credit-units required for BSCHSTAMJ1 students should complete at least 12
credit-units from each area of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics.
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF (精
神健康急救課程));
and

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Science with Honours in Science (STEAM)
through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 90 credit-units# as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-
level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 12 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry from Table 1;
2.1.1.2 21 credit-units of Integrated STEAM training in Table 2;
2.1.1.3 48 credit-units of elective courses from Table 3 of which at least 12 credit-units
shall be at 3000-level and at least 15 credit-units shall be at 4000-level;
2.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 4;
# Of the 90 credit-units required for BSCHSTAMJ2 students should complete at least 9
credit-units from each area of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics.
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Science with Honours in Science (STEAM)
through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 66 credit-units^ as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 18 credit-units of Integrated STEAM training in Table 2;
3.1.1.2 39 credit-units of elective courses from Table 3 of which at least 12 credit-units
shall be at 3000-level and 15 credit-units shall be at 4000-level;
3.1.1.3 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 4;
^ Of the 66 credit-units required for BSCHSTAMJ3 students should complete at least 6
credit-units from each area of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics.
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
