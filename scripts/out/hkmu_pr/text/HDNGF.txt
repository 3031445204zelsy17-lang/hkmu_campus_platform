Programme Requirements for
Higher Diploma in Nursing Studies (General Health Care)
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to students admitted in
2025 Autumn Term and thereafter.
1. Programme Requirement
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the Higher Diploma in Nursing Studies (General Health Care), a
student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 90 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 6 credit-units shall be at 3000-
/4000-level:
1.1.1.1 63 credit-units of Theoretical courses in Table 1; and
1.1.1.2 27 credit-units of Clinical Practicum courses in Table 2;
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF (精
神健康急救課程)) (applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after 2025/26
cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain grade “C” or above or a “Pass” grade of the programme (otherwise students must
retake the respective course(s))
and
1.1.4 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of sub-
degrees.
