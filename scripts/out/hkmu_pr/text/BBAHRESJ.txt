Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours in Real Estate and Surveying
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours in
Real Estate and Surveying through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 120 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall be
at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall
be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 60 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 3 credit-units of core strategy course from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 27 credit-units of Real Estate and Surveying concentration core courses from
Table 3;
1.1.1.4 15 credit-units of Real Estate and Surveying concentration elective courses from
Table 4, of which at least 6 credit-units from 3000-level;
1.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 5;
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
1.1.1.7 All four Global Immersion Programme courses in Table 6;
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF (精
神健康急救課程)) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after 2025/26
cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain the CGPA required for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours in
Real Estate and Surveying through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 90 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-
level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 36 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in Table 1;
2.1.1.2 3 credit-units of core strategy course from Table 2;

Table 3;
2.1.1.4 15 credit-units of Real Estate and Surveying concentration elective courses from

2.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 5;

3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours in
Real Estate and Surveying through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 66 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-
level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 12 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in Table 1;
3.1.1.2 3 credit-units of core strategy course from Table 2;
3.1.1.3 27 credit-units of Real Estate and Surveying concentration core courses from
Table 3;
3.1.1.4 15 credit-units of Real Estate and Surveying concentration elective courses from
Table 4, of which at least 6 credit-units from 3000-level.
3.1.1.5 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 5;
3.1.1.6 GIP 300BEF and GIP 400BEF of the Global Immersion Programme courses in
Table 6;
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA required for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
