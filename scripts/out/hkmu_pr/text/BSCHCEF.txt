Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Science with Honours in Computer Engineering
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to
students admitted in 2025 Autumn Term and thereafter.
1. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Science with Honours in Computer
Engineering through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 60 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-
level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 30 credit-units of core courses as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 18 credit-units of elective courses from Table 3;
1.1.1.3 an additional 3 credit-units of elective courses from either Table 2 or 3; and
1.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 4;
and
1.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
