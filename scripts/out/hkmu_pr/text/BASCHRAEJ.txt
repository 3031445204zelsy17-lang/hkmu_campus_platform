Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Applied Science with Honours in Robotics and Automation Engineering
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to the
following student cohorts:
Year of Entry Admission cohort
Year 1 2026/27 and thereafter
Year 2 2027/28 and thereafter
Year 3 2028/29 and thereafter
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Applied Science with Honours in
Robotics and Automation Engineering through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 126 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units
shall be at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24
credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 69 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in Table 1;
1.1.1.2 30 credit-units of practicum courses applicable to Year 1 entry as listed in
Table 2;
1.1.1.3 6 credit-units of elective courses in Table 3;
1.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 4;
1.1.1.5 6 credit-units of University English courses in Table 5; and
1.1.1.6 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see
www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF
(精神健康急救課程)) (applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after
2025/26 cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Applied Science with Honours in
Robotics and Automation Engineering through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 93 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 51 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in Table 1;
2.1.1.2 27 credit-units of practicum courses applicable to Year 2 entry as listed in
Table 2;
2.1.1.3 6 credit-units of elective courses in Table 3; and
2.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 4
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Applied Science with Honours in
Robotics and Automation Engineering through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 66 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 36 credit-units of core courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in Table 1;
3.1.1.2 21 credit-units of practicum courses applicable to Year 3 entry as listed in
Table 2; and
3.1.1.3 9 credit-units of University Core courses in Table 4
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
