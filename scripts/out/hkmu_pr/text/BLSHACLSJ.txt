Programme Requirements for
Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours (Applied Chinese Language Studies)
This 3-credit-unit system programme requirements document is applicable to the following student
cohorts:
Year of Entry Admission cohort
Year 1 2023/24 and thereafter
Year 2 2024/25 and thereafter
Year 3 2025/26 and thereafter
1. Programme Requirement – Year 1 Entry
1.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours (Applied
Chinese Language Studies) through Year 1 Entry, a student shall:
1.1.1 obtain 120 credit-units as prescribed below, of which no more than 30 credit-units shall
be at 1000-level, at least 24 credit-units shall be at 3000-level and at least 24 credit-units
shall be at 4000-level:
1.1.1.1 90 credit-units of core courses from Table 1;
1.1.1.2 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 2;
1.1.1.3 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 3;
1.1.1.4 3 credit-units of a periphery course from Table 4;
1.1.1.5 6 credit-units of University English language courses from Table 5;
1.1.1.6 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 6;
1.1.1.7 6 credit-units of General Education (GE) courses (see www.hkmu.edu.hk/FT_GE
for the updated list of GE courses);
and
1.1.2 complete the mandatory course on Mental Health First Aid (either the English version
NURS1050NEF (Mental Health First Aid Training) or the Chinese version NURS 1050NCF (
精神健康急救課程)) (only applicable to Year 1 entry students admitted in and after
2025/26 cohort);
and
1.1.3 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.

2. Programme Requirement – Year 2 Entry
2.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours (Applied
Chinese Language Studies) through Year 2 Entry, a student shall:
2.1.1 obtain 93 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
2.1.1.1 78 credit-units of core courses from Table 1;
2.1.1.2 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 2;
2.1.1.3 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 3;
2.1.1.4 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 6;
and
2.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award of
undergraduate degrees.
3. Programme Requirement – Year 3 Entry
3.1 To be eligible for the award of the degree of Bachelor of Language Studies with Honours
(Applied Chinese Language Studies) through Year 3 Entry, a student shall:
3.1.1 obtain 66 credit-units as prescribed below, of which at least 24 credit-units shall be at
3000-level and at least 24 credit-units shall be at 4000-level:
3.1.1.1 54 credit-units of core courses from Table 1;
3.1.1.2 3 credit-units of an elective course from Table 2;
3.1.1.3 9 credit-units of University Core courses from Table 6;
and
3.1.2 attain the CGPA for graduation as prescribed in the Regulations for the award
of undergraduate degrees.
