         General Education (GE) Courses Selection Guide

                          (For 3-credit-unit system)

This guide applies only to undergraduate students admitted under the following
academic cohorts:
- School of A&SS, B&A, E&L or S&T:
      o Year-1 entry students admitted in the 2023/24AY and thereafter
      o Year-2 entry students admitted in the 2024/25AY and thereafter
- School of N&HS:
      o BSCHMLSJ1 Year-1 entry students admitted in the 2023/24AY and
         thereafter
      o BSCHPTJ1 Year-1 entry students admitted in the 2024/25AY and
         thereafter
      o BNHGJ1 and BNHMJ1 Year-1 entry students admitted in the 2025/26 AY
         and thereafter
      o BSCHDRJ Year-1 entry students admitted in the 2026/27 AY and thereafter
You are required to complete the specific number of General Education (GE) courses
prescribed by your programme requirements. Each GE course must be chosen from a
different ‘field of study’, and these fields must differ from the ‘field(s) of study’ of
your own degree programme.

You should check your programme requirements for details for the specific number
of GE courses required, and your Advice Sheet on Course Selection – available on
the University’s website (Undergraduate & Sub-degree Students > Course Enrolment
> Advice on Course Selection) – to confirm if you are advised to enroll in any GE
courses this term.

If you are assigned a special study plan, separate communications will be sent with
further details.

            completed GE courses.




      ii.   Cross-reference your completed GE courses with the GE Course Catalog to identify
            their designated fields.

Step 3. Check 1000-level Credit-units Constraints

Review your Programme Requirements. Pay close attention to the maximum allowable credit-units
for 1000-level courses so you do not exceed the limit.


Step 4. Choose and Enrol GE Courses

    If enrolling a GE course will NOT exceed the maximum credit-unit loading for the term, you
     can self-enroll online.
    If enrolling a GE course will exceed the maximum credit-unit loading for the term, you must
     submit an Application for Add/drop during the add/drop period for adding extra courses
