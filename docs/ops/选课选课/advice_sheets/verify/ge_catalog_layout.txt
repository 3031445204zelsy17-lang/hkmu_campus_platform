                  Undergraduate General Education Courses Catalog

                                    (For 3-credit-unit only)


This course catalog provides descriptions of General Education courses offered by
Schools. It does not include Elective courses or University English courses, which are
listed on their respective webpages.


In addition to reviewing the pre-requisite requirements and excluded combinations in
this catalog, you should check your programme requirements for details for the specific
number of GE courses required, and your Advice Sheet on Course Selection – available
on the University’s website (Undergraduate & Sub-degree Students > Course
Enrolment > Advice on Course Selection) – to confirm if you are advised to enroll in
any GE courses this term.


If you are assigned a special study plan, separate communications will be sent with
further details.


Please note that only excluded combinations with face-to-face courses are shown in this
catalog; excluded combinations involving distance learning and web-based courses are
not listed.


For further enquiries regarding course content, pre-requisites or excluded combinations,
please contact the offering Schools directly.

      GEN 2019ABF Introduction to South China Studies 華南研究概論
      Credit-units:                    3
      Level:                           2000
      Medium of Instruction:           Bilingual
      Offering School:                 School of Arts and Social Sciences


      This course aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the history and development of South
      China studies, focusing on the core questions: Under the unity of Chinese culture, why and how does
      internal social differentiation occur? In terms of analytical tools and methodology, South China studies
      are influenced by the discipline of anthropology and history. In terms of sources, greater emphasis is
      placed on field investigations and the exploration and utilization of local documents. In terms of research
      trends, although South China studies initially focused on the southern regions of Fujian and Guangdong,
      they have now transcended regional boundaries and moved towards larger-scale regional comparisons.

      GEN 1012ACF Women in Hong Kong Cantonese Films 香港粵語電影中的女性

      程度:                   1000
      授課語言:                 中文
      所屬學院:                 人文社會科學院
